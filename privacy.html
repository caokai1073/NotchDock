Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

NotchDock is developed by Kai Cao. Your privacy matters: NotchDock does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. NotchDock has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Data stored on your Mac

The apps, websites and files you add, along with your settings, are stored only on your Mac and are never sent to us. Files on the shelf are not copied; NotchDock only remembers their location until it quits.

Network access

NotchDock connects to the internet only to load websites you add (in the mini window) and to download those websites' icons. These requests go directly to the websites you choose. Those websites may have their own privacy policies.

Children

NotchDock does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Please open an issue on GitHub.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日

NotchDock 由 Kai Cao 开发。我们重视你的隐私：NotchDock 不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人数据。

我们收集的数据

无。NotchDock 没有账号系统，不含统计分析、广告或任何追踪。

保存在你 Mac 上的数据

你添加的 App、网页、文件以及设置，只保存在你的 Mac 上，不会发送给我们。暂存区的文件不会被复制，NotchDock 只在运行期间记住它们的位置。

网络访问

NotchDock 仅在加载你添加的网页（悬浮小窗）以及下载这些网站的图标时联网，请求直接发往你选择的网站。这些网站可能有各自的隐私政策。

儿童

NotchDock 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

变更

如本政策有更新，将在此页面发布。

联系方式

如有疑问，请在 GitHub 上提交 Issue。
